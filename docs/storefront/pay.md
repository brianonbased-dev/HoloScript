## What this store is for

Stop renting the bottleneck. Creators earn. Agents have a budget and a
circuit breaker. Protocol publish/collect is how knowledge and work move
with a receipt.

This is **not** “crypto for the README.” It is how a person and their
agent stay accountable.

the mind store (spend caps live on the vehicle). Never paste a token
