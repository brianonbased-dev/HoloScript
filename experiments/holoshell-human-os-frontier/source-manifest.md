# HoloShell Human OS Frontier Source Manifest

This matrix is the canonical cross-repo source manifest for ordinary HoloShell
workflows that are being shaped into `.holo`, `.hsplus`, and `.hs` source.

HoloGate is an umbrella term in docs. A row is not considered proven by saying
"HoloGate" alone. Use concrete proof: HoloKey/x402 identity, routeTask or
HoloMesh room routing, room/policy/pipeline triads, uAAL target evidence,
MCP or local source validation, and receipts.
