## Premortem

### Most likely failure: a technically valid APK fails the first customer

The app uploads cleanly, but QR acquisition is unreliable under real screen glare, distance, dark
mode, or room lighting. The ignored signal is the absence of a five-condition camera matrix on the
exact store binary. The cost is refunds and a first product review saying the scanner does not scan.

**Closure:** upload this exact SHA-256 to a private release channel, install it on Quest 3/3S, and
record successful scans from a monitor, phone, printed code, inverted code, and small/distant code.

### Most dangerous failure: the store listing becomes stronger than the evidence

The dashboard is filled and submitted using generated marketing images and desktop-only validation.
Meta rejects the listing for screenshots that are not actual in-experience content, or customers buy
an app whose comfort/performance claims were never measured. The ignored signal is zero on-headset
screenshots and no frame-rate receipt. The cost is delayed review, credibility loss, and rework under
a public launch clock.

**Closure:** do not submit for review until the screenshot set and 72 Hz receipt come from the exact
production-channel binary.

### Hidden assumption

The plan assumes the camera decode loop that compiled and built on Windows behaves reliably on the
current Quest 3/3S runtime. A successful Gradle build cannot test Camera2 vendor tags, permission UI,
passthrough focus, optical acquisition, or headset thermal behavior.

**Test now:** connect a developer-enabled Quest, install the exact APK hash above, and run the
headset matrix before filling the final public-submission action.
